--- layout: default title: GenAI Projects - Sima Didari --- # GenAI Projects ## Proxy-Based Uncertainty Estimation for Improving Instruction Following in Language Models [arXiv link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06424) **Accepted at ICML 2024** We defined a novel Uncertainty-aware Reward Model (URM) for the preference training of LLMs based on Bayesian approximation to quantify the uncertainty of paired responses. We experimentally demonstrated that using URM in LLMs training boosts their instruction following capability and their policy optimization objectives. The URM based finetuning surpasses existing methods by a large margin on benchmarks such as Vicuna and MT-bench.
